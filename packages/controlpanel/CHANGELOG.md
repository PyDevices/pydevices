## v0.1.0 (unreleased)

- First release: sixteen patch pads in eight banks, sixteen encoders with
  16-LED rings, a 16 x 2 dot-matrix display, engine light, FINE, BYPASS,
  RELOAD and an optional EXIT button, in black and silver themes. It runs as
  a plain module or as an appkit app. `set_status()` puts a live readout
  on the display's second line in place of READY.
