## v0.1.0 (unreleased)

- First release: a picker for every audioinstruments instrument, played
  from every MIDI keyboard the program can reach (USB host on a board,
  Windows and Linux MIDI ports on a computer), with controlpanel as the
  instrument's front panel. Knobs sending CC 70 to 77 move the first eight
  macros, program change picks the patch, and the display shows the render
  load and the underruns since the instrument started. With no keyboard,
  the pads in bank A play notes.
